## Standby and On-Call
Employees assigned to on-call rotation must be reachable by phone within 5 minutes and able to arrive at the assigned headquarters or emergency location within 45 minutes during their on-call week. On-call employees must remain fit for duty (no alcohol consumption) for the duration of the rotation. On-call pay is 2 hours at straight-time rate per day of standby, plus overtime rates for hours actually worked on call-outs, with a minimum 2-hour call-out guarantee.

## Overtime and Rest
Employees should not work more than 16 consecutive hours. After working 16 hours, an employee is entitled to at least 8 hours of rest before returning to work; if the rest period overlaps the regular shift, the hours of overlap are paid. Supervisors may approve exceptions only during declared emergencies, and must document them.

## Fitness for Duty
Employees must report to work fit to perform their duties safely. Prescription and over-the-counter medications that may impair the ability to drive or operate equipment must be disclosed to the supervisor (the specific medication does not need to be disclosed). The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides free, confidential counseling 24/7.

## Use of Company Vehicles
Company vehicles are for business use only. Take-home vehicles are authorized for on-call and designated emergency response roles. Passengers who are not company employees or authorized contractors are not permitted. Hand-held phone use while driving is prohibited (see GS-SF-210). Report all vehicle incidents immediately to the supervisor and Fleet Services.

## Training Requirements
All Gas Operations employees must complete annual training on emergency response, hazard communication, and the Code of Conduct by December 31 each year. Operator Qualification requalifications are tracked separately. Employees will be removed from covered tasks if any required qualification lapses.

## Information Security
Gas system maps, regulator station locations, and SCADA information are classified as Confidential – Critical Infrastructure. Do not share them outside the company, post them on social media, or store them on personal devices. Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.

## Respectful Workplace
The company prohibits harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. Employees may raise concerns with their supervisor, Human Resources, or the anonymous Ethics Helpline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is grounds for discipline up to and including termination.

## Time Off
Vacation requests for more than 3 consecutive days should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. During declared emergencies or storm season staffing plans, previously approved vacation may be honored, but new requests may be deferred.
