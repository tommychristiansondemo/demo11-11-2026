# Regulatory Compliance Guide for Gas Operations
Document GS-RC-300 · Revision 3 · Owner: Gas Compliance
FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE — not an actual SDG&E document.

## 1. Regulatory Framework
Company gas operations are subject to:
- Federal pipeline safety regulations administered by the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), principally 49 CFR Part 191 (reporting), Part 192 (minimum safety standards for transportation of natural and other gas), and Part 199 (drug and alcohol testing).
- California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) General Order 112-F, which adopts and supplements the federal standards for California gas operators, and the CPUC Safety and Enforcement Division (SED) audits.
- Cal/OSHA occupational safety requirements for employees.
- Local air district rules for methane emissions and blowdowns.

## 2. Incident Reporting Timelines
When an incident occurs (release of gas with death, in-patient hospitalization, property damage of $122,000 or more as adjusted by PHMSA, or unintentional gas loss of 3 million cubic feet or more):
- PHMSA telephonic notice to the National Response Center: at the earliest practicable moment, no later than 1 hour after confirmed discovery. An update with revised estimates is required within 48 hours.
- CPUC notification: company standard is notification to the CPUC SED within 2 hours of discovery during business hours and within 4 hours outside business hours.
- Written PHMSA incident report (Form 7100.1): within 30 days of detection.
Field employees do not make regulatory notifications; they must immediately notify Gas Emergency Dispatch and their supervisor, who will engage the on-call Compliance Officer.

## 3. Safety-Related Conditions
Conditions such as general corrosion that has reduced wall thickness, unintended movement or abnormal loading by environmental causes, or a malfunction that causes pressure to rise above maximum allowable operating pressure (MAOP) plus build-up may be reportable Safety-Related Conditions. Report any such observation to Gas Compliance the same day. Compliance determines reportability; the federal report is due within 5 working days of determination, and not later than 10 working days after discovery.

## 4. Damage Prevention (Call Before You Dig)
California Government Code 4216 requires excavators to contact the regional notification center (811) at least two working days, but not more than 14 calendar days, before excavating. The company must locate and mark its facilities, or advise that no conflict exists, before the legal start date and time. Locate marks for gas are yellow. Delayed or inaccurate locates must be reported to the Damage Prevention team because they may expose the company to enforcement penalties.

## 5. Drug and Alcohol Testing (49 CFR Part 199)
Employees who perform covered functions (operations, maintenance, or emergency response on the pipeline) are subject to pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable-cause, return-to-duty, and follow-up testing. Post-accident testing must be performed as soon as practicable, and alcohol testing within 2 hours (no later than 8 hours) of the accident; drug testing within 32 hours. Supervisors must be trained in reasonable-cause observation.

## 6. Audits and Records
The CPUC SED performs periodic audits of each operating district. Records must be accurate, complete, and traceable, verifiable, and complete (TVC) — especially records that establish MAOP. Never alter, back-date, or discard a compliance record. Corrections are made by a single line through the error with initials, date, and reason. Suspected falsification must be reported to the Ethics Helpline.

## 7. Distribution Integrity Management Program (DIMP)
The company's DIMP plan identifies threats (corrosion, natural forces, excavation damage, other outside force, material or welds, equipment failure, incorrect operations, and other concerns), ranks risk, and implements additional actions such as accelerated replacement of vintage plastic and bare steel. Field employees support DIMP by accurately recording leak cause, pipe material, and condition codes on every leak repair order.
